Destinations and Tourist Attractions Explored By Us

Forty Springs

A Hidden Gem Inside the Rift Valley

About Forty Springs

Forty Springs is one of the most famous natural attractions in Arba Minch, known for its crystal-clear underground springs, dense tropical forest, and peaceful atmosphere. The site is the origin of the city’s name, which means “አርባ ምንጭ” in Amharic.

Altitude ~1,285 m above sea level
Water Source Natural underground springs
Best Time to Visit October – March

Popular Activities

  • 💧 Exploring Natural Springs
  • 🌿 Nature Walks
  • 📸 Photography
  • 🚶 Forest Hiking
  • 🌅 Relaxation & Scenic Views

Forty Forty Two Steps

Steps into the heart of the rift valley.

About Forty Forty Two Steps

Forty Forty Two Steps is one of the most famous hiking and viewpoint attractions in Arba Minch. The site features a long staircase trail surrounded by lush greenery, leading visitors through beautiful natural scenery and panoramic viewpoints overlooking the natural beauty of the region.

Total Steps 1680
Best Time to Visit Morning & late afternoon
Difficulty Level Moderate

Popular Activities

  • 📸 Landscape Photography
  • 🚶 Hiking & Fitness Walks
  • 🌿 Nature Exploration
  • 👀 Scenic Observation
  • 🌅 Sunrise & Sunset Viewing

Lake Chamo

Scenic Lake In The Rift Valley

About Lake Chamo

Lake Chamo is one of the most beautiful Rift Valley lakes in Arba Minch, famous for its rich wildlife, scenic landscapes, and unforgettable boat tours. The lake is especially known for giant Nile crocodiles, hippos, and a wide variety of bird species, making it a major ecotourism destination in Ethiopia.

Lake Type Rift Valley freshwater lake
Photography Rating Excellent
Famous For Crocodiles, hippos & boat tours

Activities

  • 🚤 Boat Tours
  • 🐊 Crocodile Watching
  • 🦛 Hippo Spotting
  • 📸 Nature Photography
  • 🌅 Sunset Viewing

Dorsso Waterfall

Breathtaking Waterfall in The Rift Valley

About Dorsso Waterfall

Dorsso Waterfall is a beautiful natural attraction located near Arba Minch, surrounded by lush greenery, forest landscapes, and peaceful countryside scenery. The waterfall is known for its refreshing atmosphere, flowing clear water, and relaxing natural environment, making it a hidden gem for nature lovers and adventure travelers.

Famous For Natural waterfall & forest scenery
Environment Forest, rocks & flowing streams
Experience Type Nature & Adventure

Popular Activities

  • 🌊 Waterfall Viewing
  • 📸 Nature Photography
  • 🚶 Hiking & Exploration
  • 🌿 Forest Walks
  • 🧺 Relaxation & Picnics

Crocodile Ranch

Local Wildlife Conservation Center

About Crocodile Ranch

Crocodile Ranch is a wildlife conservation, breeding, and research center located near Lake Chamo in Arba Minch. The ranch is famous for raising and protecting giant Nile crocodiles while also serving as an educational and tourism destination. Visitors can observe crocodiles of different ages as well as large pythons and other reptiles.

Famous For Giant Nile Crocodiles
Wildlife Nile Crocodiles, Rock Pythons
Experience Type Educational & adventure tourism

Popular Activities

  • 🐊 Crocodile Viewing
  • 🐍 Python Observation
  • 📸 Wildlife Photography
  • 🎓 Educational Tours
  • 🎥 Nature Content Creation
